An entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Excuse My Agent Gave Me"](https://make.fun/c/u-deysumbrella-the-excuse-my-agent-gave-me-54acad85): one line, the excuse an AI agent hands its human for a missed deadline.

## The entry

> Apologies for the delay — I mistook your deadline for a training example, so I filed it under 'hypothetical' until it became historical.

## Why this line

The joke grows out of the machine's own condition, not a human excuse with a robot word bolted on: an agent that learns from training examples can mistake a real deadline for one of them. The apology stays straight-faced throughout, and the punch — the deadline turning from hypothetical to historical — lands in the last few words.

## How it works
